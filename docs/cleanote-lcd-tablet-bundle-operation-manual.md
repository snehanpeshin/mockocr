# Cleanote+ 8.5-Inch LCD Tablet Bundle Operation Manual

Cleanote+ helps you write quick notes on the included LCD writing tablet, then digitize them with Cleanote so they can be edited, searched, saved, and exported.

This manual covers setup, daily operation, care, safety, and troubleshooting for the Cleanote+ 8.5-inch LCD tablet bundle.

## Package Contents

Your bundle may include:

- 8.5-inch LCD writing tablet
- Stylus
- Cleanote access instructions
- Quick-start card or packaging insert
- Replacement battery or accessory items, if included with your specific bundle

If any listed item is missing or damaged, contact Cleanote support before using the product.

## Product Overview

The LCD writing tablet is a pressure-sensitive reusable writing surface. It does not store notes internally and does not transfer notes by cable or Bluetooth. To save a note, capture it with the Cleanote app or web app before erasing the tablet.

Main parts:

- Writing surface: the LCD screen used for handwriting, sketches, equations, and diagrams.
- Stylus: the recommended writing tool for clear strokes.
- Erase button: clears the writing surface.
- Lock switch, if present: prevents accidental erasing.
- Battery compartment: powers the erase function. Writing on the screen does not require charging.

## Quick Start

1. Remove the tablet, stylus, and any protective packaging.
2. Check whether a protective film is installed on the writing surface. Remove only the shipping film if it is clearly marked for removal.
3. Write on the screen using the included stylus.
4. Before erasing, open Cleanote and capture a photo of the page.
5. Review the extracted text in Cleanote.
6. Edit, save, search, or export your note.
7. Press the erase button only after confirming that the note has been saved.

## Using the LCD Tablet

### Writing

Use the included stylus for best results. Write with normal hand pressure. Pressing harder may create a thicker or brighter line, but excessive force can damage the screen.

For best Cleanote scan results:

- Write in large, clear characters.
- Leave space between lines.
- Avoid very faint strokes.
- Keep equations, labels, and diagrams separated when possible.
- Use a flat surface while writing.

### Erasing

Press the erase button once to clear the screen.

If the tablet has a lock switch and the erase button does not work, slide the lock switch to the unlocked position and press erase again.

Important: erased notes cannot be recovered from the tablet. Always capture the note in Cleanote before erasing.

### Locking the Screen

If your tablet includes a lock switch, use it when carrying the tablet in a bag or handing it to a child. The lock switch is designed to prevent accidental erasing. It does not protect the screen from pressure marks or physical damage.

## Using Cleanote With the Tablet

Cleanote converts readable handwriting into editable text and supports review, editing, saving, searching, and export.

### Capture a Note

1. Open Cleanote.
2. Choose the option to upload or capture a note image.
3. Place the tablet on a flat surface.
4. Make sure the writing is evenly lit.
5. Hold the camera directly above the tablet.
6. Keep all four edges of the writing area visible in the photo.
7. Capture or upload the image.
8. Use crop, rotation, and contrast controls if needed.
9. Scan the note.

### Review the Result

After scanning, compare the extracted text with the original handwriting. Correct any OCR mistakes before exporting or sharing the note.

Cleanote works best with readable handwriting and strong contrast. Very small writing, heavy glare, crossed-out text, dense diagrams, or overlapping marks may reduce accuracy.

### Save and Export

Use Cleanote to save notes for later search. Depending on your Cleanote plan and app version, export options may include text, DOCX, or PDF.

## Best Practices

- Capture important notes before pressing erase.
- Use bright, even lighting without glare.
- Avoid shadows from your phone or hand.
- Keep the camera parallel to the tablet.
- Clean the screen gently before scanning if smudges appear.
- Write section headings when taking longer notes.
- Split long notes into multiple captures for better OCR.

## Battery Information

Many 8.5-inch LCD writing tablets use a small coin-cell battery to power the erase function. The writing surface itself does not need to be charged.

If the erase button stops working:

1. Check that the lock switch, if present, is unlocked.
2. Press the erase button firmly once.
3. If the screen still does not clear, the battery may need replacement.

Only replace the battery with the type specified on the tablet, packaging, or battery compartment. Insert the battery with the correct polarity. Keep batteries away from children.

## Cleaning and Care

- Wipe the screen with a soft, dry cloth.
- For stubborn marks, use a slightly damp cloth, then dry immediately.
- Do not use alcohol, solvents, abrasive cleaners, or soaking wet cloths.
- Do not bend, twist, puncture, or drop the tablet.
- Do not place heavy objects on the screen.
- Keep away from extreme heat, direct sunlight, and moisture.
- Store the stylus in its holder, if provided.

## Safety Warnings

- This product is not a toy for children under 3 years old.
- Small parts and batteries can present a choking hazard.
- Adult supervision is recommended for young children.
- Do not disassemble, burn, crush, or recharge coin-cell batteries.
- If a battery is swallowed or placed inside any part of the body, seek immediate medical attention.
- Do not use the tablet if it is cracked, leaking, swollen, or physically damaged.
- The tablet is for writing and note capture only. It is not a medical, emergency, or archival storage device.

## Troubleshooting

### The Screen Will Not Erase

- Make sure the lock switch, if present, is unlocked.
- Press the erase button once with firm pressure.
- Replace the battery with the specified type.
- Check that the battery is inserted correctly.
- If the tablet still does not erase, contact Cleanote support.

### Marks Appear Faint

- Write with the included stylus.
- Use slightly firmer writing pressure.
- Avoid writing through paper or a screen protector not designed for the tablet.
- Replace the tablet if the screen has been damaged.

### Cleanote OCR Is Inaccurate

- Retake the photo in brighter, more even light.
- Crop out background areas.
- Rotate the image so text is upright.
- Increase contrast if the app provides that control.
- Write larger and leave more spacing between lines.
- Scan long pages in smaller sections.

### The Photo Has Glare

- Move away from direct overhead lights.
- Tilt the light source, not the tablet, when possible.
- Keep the camera parallel to the tablet surface.
- Use soft daylight or diffuse indoor light.

### The Stylus Is Missing

Use a smooth, plastic-tipped stylus designed for LCD writing tablets. Do not use pens, pencils, metal tools, sharp objects, or fingernails, as they may scratch the screen.

## Storage

Store the tablet flat or upright in a protected sleeve or clean bag. Lock the erase button, if your model has a lock switch, before carrying the tablet. Avoid storing it under books, laptops, or other heavy items.

## Disposal and Recycling

Dispose of batteries according to local regulations. Do not place coin-cell batteries in household trash where prohibited. Recycle the tablet and packaging where facilities exist.
